Questions we get from Mumbai customers

Is cold pressed oil the same as wood pressed oil?

Not quite. Cold pressed means the oil was pressed without added heat, usually on a steel expeller. Wood pressed is a slower kind of cold pressing that uses a wooden ghani, which generates less friction heat than steel. All wood pressed oil is cold pressed; not all cold pressed oil is wood pressed.

We are a small pressing unit in Govandi rather than a shop floor, so message or call before coming and we will tell you what is pressed and ready.

Why is it more expensive than the oil in my local shop?

A wooden ghani yields far less oil from the same seed than an industrial solvent plant, and it runs much more slowly. You are paying for lower yield and unrefined quality, not for packaging.

Why does my oil look cloudy, or have sediment at the bottom?

That is normal and expected in unrefined oil. Sediment is the settled solids of the seed. Coconut oil also solidifies below roughly 24 °C, which in a Mumbai winter is common. Neither is a fault.
